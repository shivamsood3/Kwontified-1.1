Questions executives ask

What does Kwontified do?

Kwontified manages online marketplaces for consumer brands. One senior team plans and runs your advertising, creative, catalog, inventory and reporting on Amazon, Walmart, Target Plus and five more marketplaces as a single business.

Which marketplaces does Kwontified manage?

Amazon Seller Central and Vendor Central, Walmart Marketplace and Walmart Supplier, Target Plus, Zalando, Mercado Libre, Temu, TikTok Shop and Instagram Shop. We also prepare product data for AI shopping assistants such as Claude, ChatGPT, Alexa for Shopping, Walmart Sparky and Google Gemini.

Which product categories does Kwontified serve?

Kwontified works across hardlines, softlines and consumables. In hardlines that includes consumer electronics, small appliances and housewares. In softlines it includes apparel, footwear, handbags, luggage, accessories and jewelry. We also manage beauty and personal care, health and wellness, home goods, and consumer packaged goods such as food and beverage, household, baby and pet products.

What is included in marketplace management?

Six capabilities under one plan: retail media and advertising, creative, marketplace operations, analytics and forecasting, agentic commerce, and revenue recovery on Amazon. You approve one set of sales, margin, advertising and inventory targets.

How is this different from hiring separate agencies?

Most brands split advertising, creative and operations across separate agencies. Here one named team owns the plan and the work, so budget moves between channels on measured return and catalog work is done once and adapted for each platform.

How often will we hear from you?

You get a weekly update, plus monthly and quarterly business reviews against the plan you approved. Senior leaders stay involved long after onboarding.
